# Familiarity with the benefits

As well as being familiar with the central ideas, campaigns should also build awareness of the benefits of a giftmoot economy, so that people are more likely to passively and actively support its implementation.  The benefits of a giftmoot economy are very broad, but it may be worthwhile to begin by targeting specific benefits at specific audiences and expanding once they have engaged.

Here I've put together a basic proposal of what benefits people of different political alignments might find attractive or relevant.  I assume that there are other, perhaps better, ways of making targeted approaches, but I'll leave this here as a starting point.


## Progressives

I've here lumped together some political positions that are often associated, but by no means always completely aligned, and considered what parts of a giftmoot economy might appeal to them.

### Cost-of-living

Cost-of-living issues are an issue identified by people from multiple demographics and political orientations, rather than being the exclusive domain of "progressives".  However, I've put it here in relation to some notion of general distributive justice - that is, that the cost-of-living is a general issue that should be addressed for everyone, rather than a specific demographic - which is perhaps more commonly associated with some groups of progressives.

A giftmoot economy lowers wealth inequality and enables a better provision of essentials, ensuring that someone is not barred access from essential goods because of their ability to save or work.

### Environmentalism

Because a giftmoot economy does not require indefinite growth, it can be a more sustainable economic model.  In addition, businesses are not incentivised to push products, take cost-lowering measures that cause pollution, or make their products have planned obsolescence.

### Feminism

A significant amount of unpaid labour is performed by women and goes unrecognised or unremunerated in a market economy, which can also cause financial dependence on others and constrain individual security and autonomy.  A giftmoot economy recognises all work as work by not dividing work into "economically productive" and "domestic" spheres.

### Workplace conditions and business practices

Businesses are not incentivised to cut costs in a manner that exploits labour, because labour can freely leave.  In addition, businesses are not incentivised to produce unsafe products or engage in unsafe or exploitative business practices.

## Socialists

Here I'll lump together various sorts of socialists.  Despite many disagreements about how socialism should be practiced or what constitutes socialism, the following are likely to appeal in most cases.

### Labour empowerment

Because labour can freely leave workplaces, and because workplaces require labour in order to operate, labour has the power to dictate the conditions and purposes of workplaces.  Labour will not participate in a cause that they do not believe in, and will not work under conditions they find problematic.  While this is not the same as labour owning the means of production, there is a strong overlap in outcomes here.

### Fairness and equality

A giftmoot economy would see an inability to accrue abstract or excessive wealth, which would lead to increased equality.  It would also ensure that people could engage with the economy without a disparity of power based on finances.  There are also concerns from socialists that excessive wealth and exclusion from the economy are an indicator of a lack of fairness.

### Common ownership and democracy

Many socialists advocate for private property to be converted to commonly owned property.  While a giftmoot economy does not make a particular distinction between resources that are the means of production and those that are not, and it does not make a particular normative claim about thecommon ownership of property, giftmoots themselves are democratic organisations that can receive and distribute resources and thus parallel some notions of commonly owned property.

## Neoliberals

Here I'll lump together people who generally believe that markets solve problems best.  There are perhaps three main things that this type of belief rests on: individual liberty in economic interactions, private property, and price-signalling.  The implications of these are things like small government with little intevention in the market, and that markets produce more efficient results than other types of economic structure.

### Individualism and private property

A giftmoot economy preserves private property and individual liberty - it does not coerce or mandate that anyone work in any particular manner or industry, nor that they can or cannot own any particular piece of property, nor that they can or cannot use that property in any particular manner.  Individuals can collaborate as they see fit.  In fact, a giftmoot economy increases the liberty of an individual in relation to work in general by removing coercive forces.

### Economic efficiency

A giftmoot economy is likely a more efficient use of economic resources than a market economy because a market economy produces a quality of life deadweight loss.

### Small government

Because essential service provision is more accessible in a giftmoot economy, and because businesses are not incentivised to pollute, exploit workers, produce unsafe or misleading products, or similar, the amount of regulation and intervention in the economy by the government would be reduced.

### No taxes

In a non-monetary economy, the government would not have a budget through which they would collect taxes or spend money.

## Conservatives

Here I lump together groups that are focused on the moral fabric of society as part of creating cohesion and societal harmony.

### Local community institutions

Many conservatives are more suspicious not just of big government, but also the erasure of local identity, the sense of local community, and civic participation.  A giftmoot economy focuses on economic and distribution levels at a local level and allows for communities with strong community identities to be the major players in economic decision-making.

### Moral engagement with work and society

Many conservatives focus on moral character and moral responsibility.  A giftmoot economy suggests that moral principles can be compromised by pay conditions and recentres work motivation on individual moral principles and desire to contribute.

## Responses to AI

AI is often seen as a threat to a well-functioning economy because it can create conditions of mass unemployment; a giftmoot economy changes the relationship between employment and survival so that this is not a threat.
